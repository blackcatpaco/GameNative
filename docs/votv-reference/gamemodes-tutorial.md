# Gamemodes/Tutorial

The Tutorial is a playable gamemode in Voices of the Void , which primarily serves to instruct new players on the basic mechanics of the game.

Playing the tutorial through to the end is required to unlock the Story, Infinite, and Ambience gamemodes.

## Description

The Tutorial level takes place in an enclosed area similar to the Poolrooms, with ceramic tiles lining every floor, wall, and ceiling, pools of water in most places, as well as small windows letting in bright white lighting.

## Secrets

### Layer 1

#### Elevated Hallway

In the Stage 1 area, a raised platform with a doorway leading to a bright hallway can be seen. The player may drag the nearby metal table over to the platform and use it to vault up onto it, allowing entry into the hallway. At the end of this hallway, a note is attached to the wall.

When the player turns around, nothing happens.

The wall that the note is attached to is able to be destroyed with a physics item or the crowbar found in Stage 2, revealing more dark hallway. At the end of the hallway is a door leading to a large, flooded atrium, with stairs descending into a central gazebo-like structure. A hint node in front of the structure reads:

If the player attempts to swim down into the central hole, death awaits.

Above the central hole is another hint node.

- In older versions of the game, entering this hallway would spawn Spider Argemia to jumpscare and kill the player .

#### Hidden White Door

In the first room of Stage 3, one of the pillars between the white windows is marked with graffiti. Jumping into the window on the right will lead the player into a pure white room containing a single white door. The door cannot be opened.

#### Debug TP Zone

Pressing the "Debug TP" button ( Numpad . ) at any time will send the player to an otherwise inaccessible area. The player appears on a circular platform in a small, flooded room.

The door leads to a great hall with pillars and water fountains along the walls. The third fountain on the right contains bones. The fourth fountain on the left does not work.

#### Hidden Texts

Hidden markers with hint text can be found in various places throughout the tutorial.

- Upon standing in the water in the north-east corner of the starting room.
- Upon climbing into the third narrow window on the far wall of Stage 1.
- Upon standing in the smaller pool to the right of the computer table in Stage 3.
- Upon standing in the corner with the stairs in the second room of Stage 3.
- Upon climbing into the center window above the console in the second room of Stage 3.

#### The Secret Route

All mainline tutorial markers beyond the introductory one can be completely ignored. Simply take the keycard from the ledge and use it to open the door to Stage 2.

Take the crowbar from the table.

Enter '1234' into the next keypad to unlock the door, and take the waterslides down to Stage 3. On top of the end of the blue waterslide is a radioactive capsule. Throw an item at it to dislodge it and collect it. It will not irradiate the player.

- Use the "Show throw trajectory" keybind for faster acquiring.

Move ahead to the transformer room, and climb on top on it. A key is hidden on top of the support beam on the far end of the room, which the player can obtain by jumping and grabbing it.

Head back to the previous room, and climb on top of the servers. Use the crowbar to break down the wall with the drawings, revealing a secret vent. Climb in.

While crawling through the vents, a vent cover will eventually break open underneath the player, forcing them to fall into a large funnel.

The player will be dropped into a large open room with two stories.

Beyond this point, entering ragdoll mode will send the player back to the start. Be careful.

In the doorway immediately to the right, a pile of rocks can be found. Collect as many as possible.

The stairs found beyond each doorway allow the player to access the upper level of the room.

Each of the four corners of the upper level house a stone pedestal, each with a different shape. Each pedestal has a corresponding stone slab hidden in various places throughout the room that must be placed on top of them.

- The square slab can be found behind the third central pillar. Press and hold E on it to carry it, and place it on top of the square pedestal in the north-east corner (the compass still works here).
- The pentagon slab can be found on the ledge of a high window in the eastern doorway section. Throw an item (such as a rock) at it to dislodge it, and carry it to the pentagon pedestal in the south-east corner.
- The hexagon slab can be found in the south corner of the western doorway section. Carry it to the hexagon pedestal in the south-west corner.
- The triangle slab can be found on top of the vent cover high up on the south side of the room. Items must be precisely thrown at it from the side to get it down (such as the rocks). When successfully dislodged, carry it to the triangle pedestal in the north-west corner. Use the "Show throw trajectory" keybind for faster acquiring. Once obtained, the rocks will no longer be necessary.

With each slab that is returned to its pedestal, a beam will protrude out from the southernmost central pillar, allowing the player to parkour up and into the nearby vent.

On the other side of the vent is a small octagonal shaft. Find the ladder on the wall above the vent's exit, and climb up. Enter the second vent.

The player will fall into a large dirt hole. At the bottom of the hole is a second radioactive capsule to collect. A collection of bones can also be found around the pit, spelling out a sequence of numbers in Roman numerals. Starting at the corner from which the player fell and going clockwise: IV - I - III - V - II .

At this point, the player may safely press C to enter ragdoll mode, which will teleport them back to the start of the tutorial.

In the Stage 1 area, the third and final radioactive capsule can be found in the circular window above the door. It can be dislodged by throwing an item at it (such as a rock), or by using the elevated platform to walk on top of the doorframe and collect it from there.

Back in the small starting room, one of the top-rightmost wall tiles on the back wall can be interacted with ( E ) to reveal a keyhole. Hold the key collected from earlier and press LMB on the keyhole to insert it, then press E to unlock it.

Unlocking the keyhole will open up a metal grate blocking a small passage in the pool of the Stage 1 area. Crouch and swim into it.

Beyond the narrow, flooded passage is a small room with a vent cover.

Break open the vent with the crowbar and proceed.

Beyond the vent is a small room leading to an elevated hallway with a door.

- Due to an oversight, this door may be unpowered due to the transformer being knocked down from collecting the key. If this is the case, the player can simply open the door to proceed.
- If the door is powered, it will be locked. The password for the keypad is what was revealed by the bones in the pit. 41352

Proceed down the spiral staircase.

Through the doorway and down the stairs is a room containing a raised platform with three small holes in front of a metal grate. The three radioactive capsules collected earlier must be placed into the holes to open the grate. Swim down through it.

On the other side of the passage is a very small room with only a second hole in the ground. Jump into it.

Welcome to Layer 2.

#### Capsule Shortcut

A faster method exists of obtaining all three radioactive capsules instantly, which involves causing an explosion in the small room in which the player starts the tutorial .

### Layer 2

#### The Route

The player enters Layer 2 by falling down through a narrow stone hole into the water. Continue swimming through the tunnels.

At the end of the tunnels is the entrance to a maze.

- It is possible to make it to the end of the maze by hugging either the left or right walls.
- Monstrous groans may be heard as the player attempts to navigate the maze...

At the end of the maze is a large, dark, flooded room divided into smaller areas by arches. On the northern wall is a ladder.

Ascending the ladder leads to a large room with large pipes along the walls. The player must climb on top of the lower section of pipes on the far side of the room to parkour over to the elevated area.

In the elevated area is a narrow hallway leading to another room with columns and two sectioned-off pool areas.

On the back of one of the columns, one of the tiles protrudes out slightly. Pressing E to interact with the tile causes a ladder to descend from the ceiling.

At the top of this ladder is the entrance to a vent shaft, which leads to a second ladder.

This ladder leads to a room with five large windows angled down towards the pool.

Climbing into the window on the far side of the room reveals a hole that the player must drop down into.

The player is dropped into a pool at the center of a panopticon, with rusted metal doors surrounding the room on multiple levels.

None of the metal doors can be opened. The exit is an open hallway on the eastern side of the second level.

The hallway leads to a sectioned off room with metal barred windows overlooking the pipes room from earlier.

Two of the metal bars in the leftmost window can be dislodged by repeatedly grabbing them and pressing LMB to throw them, allowing the player to walk out onto a ledge.

To the left is a vent cover that must be broken. If the player has lost their crowbar, one can be found at the right from the vent in one of the 3 pipes above.

Continue through to the end of the vents; none of the vent covers are destructible until the end. Destroy the final vent cover and drop down.

The player falls into another sectioned off room overlooking the pipes room. Continue through the narrow doorway.

The player emerges in a very large, open room with ample skylight access, two large pools of differing elevations, and an eastern wall filled with large alcoves.

The player must carefully jump and climb between the alcoves on the wall without falling to reach the one with blood leaking out of it. At the back of the bloody alcove is a vent cover which can be broken into.

Continue through the vent and break the cover to drop down. The player will fall through a second vent cover and continue falling down through a long vertical vent shaft.

Welcome to Layer 3.

#### Secret Courtyard

Hidden somewhere deep within the maze is a prison-like concrete structure with skylight access and a dead tree at the end. The doors cannot be opened.

#### Hidden Metal Door

In the large room with pipes, it is possible to climb on top of the pipes leading to the right-most window. Inside the window is a secret passage to a drop into a pool of water. The only thing that can be accessed at this point is a nearby metal door which cannot be opened.

#### Jolly Plush

In the room with the two sectioned-off pools and dropdown ladder, a breakable wall is hidden just above one of the pools. Entering the newly revealed passage will lead the player to a small room containing the Jolly Wangcore plush and the award star. Entering this room also nets the player the "7 hours" achievement , and unlocks the Jolly plush and award star in the Store for all other playthroughs.

#### Secret Alcove

In the large, open room with the alcoves on the wall, if the player decides to continue jumping and climbing past the bloody alcove without falling, the final alcove contains a note.

When the player turns around, nothing happens.

### Layer 3

#### The Route

The player enters Level 3 by falling down a very long ventilation shaft, busting through several vent covers along the way, until finally landing in a small pool in a room at the bottom.

Continue down either stairway to reach a narrow hallway. The hallway gives way to a large cylindrical chamber.

The exit is located directly above the entrance, with a short ladder leading into it. The player must find and press the extruded tile on the wall of the inner cylindrical structure to move the ladder back and forth along the outer wall. On the second press, the player must quickly run up to the stairs and grab onto the ladder as it is moving back to the exit.

Should the player succeed, they may proceed to a small room with a blue waterslide.

Taking the waterslide lands the player in a room with pipes covering the walls. To progress, the player must climb and jump between the pipes in order to turn 4 valves and flood the room.

Once the room is flooded, the player will be able to reach a vent cover in the corner. If the player has lost their crowbar, one can be found stuck in the pipes just below the vent cover.

Crawling through the vents, the player will eventually end up in a new area with a balcony and a living room.

This is the end of the secret route.

#### The Showers

In the pipe room, the metal door on the right can be dislodged by slamming a heavy physics prop (such as a wooden crate) against it repeatedly. Doing so allows the player access to a communal showers area.

## Trivia

The sound effects and music that play when one is summoned will still play.
